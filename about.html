My story

I'm a software engineer with a passion for building technology that solves real-world problems. I studied Computer Science at the University of California, Santa Cruz, where I developed a foundation in software engineering, problem-solving, and application development. Along the way, I've had the opportunity to contribute to engineering teams at Kevala and TutorD, gaining experience across software development, backend systems, databases, APIs, and cloud technologies. Those experiences shaped how I approach technology: understand the problem first, build a practical solution, and make sure it actually makes someone's work easier. Today, I bring that engineering experience to businesses and organizations that want to get more out of technology. Whether it's building a website, automating repetitive tasks, connecting business systems, or developing custom software, my goal is to create solutions that help businesses operate smarter. I've applied that approach through projects with Westside MAPS, Kia Todd Talent Agency, and Bods by Todd.

When I was a kid, I loved building things. I built a website for my dad's business when I was 12, and by the time I was in high school I was making websites for local businesses and nonprofits. I learned how to code by building real things that people actually used.

In college I studied computer science at UC Santa Cruz, where I learned how to build software that scales and is maintainable. I also learned how to work with teams, communicate clearly, and understand the business side of technology.

Today I put that experience to work for local businesses and organizations. I've rebuilt Westside MAPS's website and the tools behind it, built a talent directory for Kia Todd Talent Agency, and launched a new site for Bods by Todd. My goal is always the same: understand how a business actually runs, then make technology do more of the work.
