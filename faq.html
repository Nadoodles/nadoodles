Pricing & timing

How much does a project cost?

It depends on what you need. A website redesign is a very different project from a custom member portal. After our first call, you'll get a written plan with a clear, fixed price before any work starts. No hourly surprises.

How long does it take?

You'll get a timeline along with your plan, based on the scope we agree on. While I'm building, you'll see progress along the way, so you always know where things stand.

Is the first call really free?

Yes. It's 30 minutes. We'll look at your website, your day-to-day processes, and the tools you use. You'll leave with three recommendations: one quick win, one thing to automate, and one bigger opportunity, whether or not we work together.

Working together

Do I need to be technical?

Not at all. Tell me how your business works and what's frustrating you, and I'll handle the technology. I explain things in plain language and you approve everything before it goes live.

What if I already have a website?

That's a great starting point. We can keep what's working, fix what isn't, and add what's missing. If it makes more sense to rebuild, I'll move your content over and handle the switch so nothing breaks.

Who owns the website when it's done?

You do. Your domain, your content, and your accounts stay in your name, so you're never locked in.

What happens after launch?

I don't disappear. I offer ongoing care for updates, content changes, security, and quick fixes. If something comes up, you know exactly who to call.

Do you only work with businesses in Los Angeles?

I'm based in LA and happy to meet in person locally, but I can work with businesses anywhere over video calls.

Technology & AI

Can you work with the tools I already use?

Usually, yes. Often the best answer isn't new software. It's connecting what you already have, like your website, payments, scheduling, email, and customer records, so information flows on its own.

What can automation actually do for my business?

It takes over the repetitive tasks: replying to new inquiries, sending appointment and payment reminders, onboarding new customers, and putting together reports. You get the time back for the work only you can do.

Is AI safe to use in my business?

When it's set up carefully, yes. I build AI tools that handle routine work, like answering common questions or sorting inquiries, with clear limits. Anything sensitive or important still comes to you for approval.
